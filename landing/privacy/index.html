Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data SpenDrip collects, why we need it, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it. It applies to spendrip.com, the SpenDrip app at app.spendrip.com, and our emails and messages.

1. Who we are

SpenDrip is operated by Magenta Pass (“SpenDrip”, “we”, “us”), a Nigerian business. We are the data controller for the personal data described here, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Questions or requests about your data: hello@spendrip.com with “Privacy” in the subject, or WhatsApp 0802 244 8089.

3. Why we may use it (legal basis)

  • Contract: to provide the service you signed up for, such as sending your scheduled transfers.
  • Legal obligation: to verify identity, keep financial records, and report suspicious activity as anti-money-laundering and other laws require.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure, prevent fraud, and improve SpenDrip, in ways you would reasonably expect.
  • Consent: for optional things, such as WhatsApp messages to the people you pay, Auto-fill, and marketing emails. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app or by contacting us.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with the companies that help us run SpenDrip, under contracts that require them to protect it, and only as much as each one needs:

  • Paystack (payments): card and bank payments, saved-card tokens, bank account name checks, BVN validation and transfers.
  • Anthropic (identity check): reads your ID photo and selfies to complete the check. The images are not used to train their models.
  • Mailgun (email delivery) and our WhatsApp messaging provider (messages to you, and to the people you pay when you turn it on).
  • DigitalOcean (servers and database) and Netlify (website and app hosting).
  • Banks and payment networks that carry each transfer, including the recipient’s bank.

We also share data when the law requires it, for example with the Nigerian Financial Intelligence Unit, regulators, courts or the police, and to protect our users or SpenDrip from fraud or harm. If SpenDrip is sold or merged, your data may pass to the new owner, who must keep this policy’s protections.

5. Data stored outside Nigeria

Our servers are in the United States, and some of the companies above process data in the United States or the European Union. When data leaves Nigeria we rely on the safeguards the NDPA allows, including contracts with these companies that require protection at least equal to Nigerian law.

6. How long we keep it

  • Identity and transaction records: at least 5 years after your account closes, because anti-money-laundering rules require it.
  • Account and plan data: while your account is open, then as above.
  • One-time sign-in codes: stored only as a hash, and expire within minutes.
  • Website and app analytics, including session recordings: up to 12 months.

When we no longer need data, we delete it or make it anonymous.

7. Cookies, analytics and session recordings

  • Necessary cookies only: the app uses a sign-in cookie and a security (CSRF) cookie. Without them you can’t stay signed in. We don’t use advertising cookies.
  • Analytics: we use Umami, which we host ourselves. It counts visits without cookies and without tracking you across other websites.
  • Session recordings: to find and fix confusing screens, we may record how pages are used (clicks, scrolling, page changes). In the app, all text and numbers on screen are hidden in recordings, and taps on the PIN pad aren’t recorded. Recordings stay on our own analytics server.
  • Your device: the app remembers some preferences, such as light or dark mode, in your browser’s storage.

If we add advertising or third-party tracking in future, we will ask for your consent first and update this policy.

8. How we protect it

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), PINs and ID numbers are stored as one-way hashes, card tokens are encrypted, access to production systems is limited to the people who need it, and every movement of money is recorded in a double-entry ledger. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission as the law requires. More on how SpenDrip keeps your money and identity safe.

9. Your rights

Under the NDPA you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your data, where we don’t have to keep it by law;
  • restrict or object to some uses, including direct marketing;
  • move your data to another service in a common format;
  • have a person review a decision made automatically. The identity check is automated; if it fails and you think it’s wrong, ask us and someone will look at it.

Write to hello@spendrip.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

10. Data about the people you pay

When you add someone, you give us their bank details and, if you choose, their WhatsApp number. Please only add people who expect to receive money from you, and tell them you use SpenDrip. Recipients can ask us to stop WhatsApp messages or to see the data we hold about them.

11. Children

SpenDrip accounts are for people aged 18 and over. You can send money to a child’s account, but we don’t knowingly create accounts for children.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we will tell you by email or in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
