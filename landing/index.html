Safety

Your money, checked twice.

Every payment is confirmed with the bank before it counts, and nothing is ever sent twice.

🔒

Your card stays with Paystack

You pay on Paystack's secure page. SpenDrip never sees your card number; we keep only an encrypted token for one-tap top-ups.

🙂

Face ID and PIN

Open the app with Face ID or your PIN. It locks itself when you step away, and five wrong PINs lock the account.

✓

Money is set aside before each transfer and only counted as sent when the bank confirms it. If a transfer fails, it comes straight back to your balance.

🪪

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does it cost?

₦50 for each drip that goes out. Topping up by bank transfer to your SpenDrip account has no SpenDrip fee. Card top-ups go through Paystack, and their card fee is shown before you pay.

What happens if I don't have enough money?

Your priorities are paid first, in the order you set. Other drips wait, and we tell you the exact amount to top up so everything goes out. Nothing is sent from money you don't have.

Who can I send money to?

Yourself and anyone with a Nigerian bank or wallet account: GTBank, Access, OPay, Kuda, Moniepoint and more. We check the account name before you save someone.

When does the money arrive?

At the time you choose, Lagos time. Transfers usually land within minutes, and if the person has WhatsApp turned on, they get a message when it does.

Can I change or stop a plan?

Any time. Edit the amount, day, time or person, pause a single plan, or pause everything with one switch. Past payments stay in your activity.

Is SpenDrip available now?

We're in early access. Join the waitlist and we'll send you an invite. If you already have access, open the app at app.spendrip.com.
