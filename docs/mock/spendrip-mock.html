Jump to

Built for ease

  • A plan reads like a sentence. Tap any coloured word to change it.
  • Good defaults, so most plans take three or four taps.
  • Every warning comes with its fix: one tap to top up the exact amount.
  • Up to 3 priorities, paid in order. Before you change the order, you see what the new order will be.
  • Plain words: "waits for top-up", never "insufficient funds".
  • Account names are checked as you type, so money can't go to the wrong person.
  • Fees are always included in the totals. No surprises.
  • Three looks: Themed, Light and Dark. Switch from the half-moon button on Home.
  • Profile (your initials, top-right of Home) holds verification, security, limits and notifications.
  • Sign-up is just three steps: your NIN, a photo of your ID, and a selfie. Then a texted code, a PIN and Face ID, and you're in.
  • Same app on the web: a sidebar, two-column screens, and dialogs in the middle of the screen. Use the Phone / Web switch at the top.
