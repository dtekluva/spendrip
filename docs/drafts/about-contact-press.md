## About page (`/about/`)

**Title:** About SpenDrip: scheduled transfers built in Nigeria
**Description:** SpenDrip is a Nigerian app for scheduled transfers. Set money to go out on the day and time you choose, to yourself or the people you look after, with every fee shown first.

### H1: Money that shows up on time

**Lead:** SpenDrip is a Nigerian app for scheduled transfers. You top up one balance and set "drips": payments that go out by themselves, daily, weekly or monthly, to your own account or to anyone with a Nigerian bank or wallet account.

### Why we built it

Most of us send the same money again and again: Mum's monthly upkeep, a sibling's school allowance, fuel for the week, money moved out of the salary account before it disappears. The banking apps can send money, but only when you remember to, and remembering is the hard part. A late transfer means a call asking "have you sent it?", and a forgotten one means someone you look after goes without.

SpenDrip does the remembering for you. You write the plan once as one sentence:

> Send **₦30,000** to **Mum** every **month** on the **25th** at **9:00 AM**.

The money goes out on time, and Mum gets a WhatsApp message when it lands.

### What we believe

- **Show every naira.** Before you start a plan, you see the SpenDrip fee, the transfer fee and stamp duty as separate lines. Nothing extra is added later. [Our fees](/fees/)
- **Protect what matters most.** Rank up to three drips as priorities. Their money is set aside for the month, so smaller drips wait instead of spending it. [How priorities work](/priorities/)
- **Count it as sent only when the bank says so.** A transfer is counted as sent only after the bank confirms it. If it fails, the money goes back to your balance. [Security](/security/)
- **Know who we're dealing with.** Everyone verifies with an ID photo and live selfies before money moves. Every payee's account name is checked before it's saved.

### How SpenDrip works behind the scenes

- **Card payments** are processed by Paystack. SpenDrip never sees your card number.
- **Transfers** are sent through Paystack to Nigerian banks and wallets.
- **Your balance** is recorded in a double-entry ledger, so every movement can be traced.
